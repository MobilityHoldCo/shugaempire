FAQ

Got Questions?

Everything you need to know about SHUGA Empire HoldCo, SHUGA FLEET, Shuga Ride, and Shuga Energy.

SHUGA FLEET

What is SHUGA FLEET?+

SHUGA FLEET is our vehicle hire-purchase programme. Qualified drivers can access an electric vehicle by paying an agreed deposit and making fixed daily payments, working toward full ownership over the agreed term.

How do I apply for a Shuga Car?+

You can apply by contacting our team through the contact page. We'll guide you through the qualification and onboarding process.

How long does it take to own the car?+

The ownership period depends on the agreement terms set at the start of the programme. Our team will outline the full terms when you apply.

Can investors own vehicles and have them operated?+

Yes. Investors can acquire vehicles and have them operated by professional drivers through our programme, with SHUGA Empire HoldCo coordinating the operational side.

Shuga Ride

Where is Shuga Ride currently available?+

Shuga Ride is currently live in Lagos and Abuja, with plans to expand to more major Nigerian cities.

How do I book a ride?+

You can book through the Shuga Ride app. Contact our team for access and download details.

Can I drive for Shuga Ride?+

Yes. Drivers who have been onboarded through the SHUGA FLEET programme can operate through Shuga Ride. Contact our team to learn more.

Shuga Energy

What is Shuga Energy?+

Shuga Energy is our solar-powered EV charging infrastructure. We operate charging hubs for Shuga drivers and for the wider public with any EV.

Can non-Shuga drivers use the charging hubs?+

Yes. Our charging network is open to the public — any EV, any operator can access our hubs.

Are the hubs solar-powered?+

Yes. Our hubs are designed to leverage solar energy to provide more sustainable, reliable charging infrastructure.

Still have questions?

Our team is happy to help. Reach out and we'll get back to you.
